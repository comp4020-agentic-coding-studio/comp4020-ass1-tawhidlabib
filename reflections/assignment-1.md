# Assignment 1 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I wanted to create a "Game of Life" game that would highlight how lucky we are
to have certain high quality of life aspects in our society such as access to clean food and water, healthcare
and electricity. I initially believed the idea was too hard to complex but claude suggested a "Birth Lottery"
game and found credible statistics on quality of life in each country in addition
to a method of calculating chances of being born in each country.

The breakthrough was how quickly the idea could become something on screen.
For example, using the Claude agent, the gap between a basic image of the world map 
and a visually appealing, working rotating globe animtion was one prompt rather than a weekend. 
That speed changed what I was willing to imagine. 

As testing ideas were quick and easy, I was able to try different things and make a better user experience.
For example, the stat cards moved off the page and onto the city image as
clickable dots, the generic skyline became a real photo of each capital pulled
from Wikipedia, and a cool landing screen appeared to explain the concept. Three
redesigns were completed in a few hours, each better than the last, and each only possible
because the previous one cost so little.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

My job shifted from slowly writing the code to bring ideas to life to instead focusing on the architecture. 
I spent my time on the design, such as where the dots sit, what the landing screen says, whether landing in a country offers a cool user experience/journey and directing the agent towards that, rather than on syntax.

That makes verification the skill that matters most. The agent was always able to quickly give me
something that passes every check, as mentioned in my process.md, a canvas sized from a hidden 0×0 box and
an invisible overlay swallowing every dot click both cleared typecheck,
build, lint and 35 tests while the page was still visibly broken. I want to be a
developer who delivers quality products and services quicker with the use of AI agents, without sacrificing any quality.
